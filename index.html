How it works

1. Build a picture sentence

Who, does what, to what, where. Big emoji tiles, no reading required. A 4-year-old can do it alone.

2. Tap Make. Play your game.

Thirty seconds later there is a 3D game that your kid made. One button to play, so little hands can do it in the car.

3. Earn upgrades by learning

Locked tiles show a padlock. Count to 10, sound out a word, or solve a puzzle to unlock. Never a checkout.

Every upgrade is a challenge, not a purchase

Other kids' apps sell coins. Game Maker asks your kid to count to ten, find a letter, or spot the odd one out. New worlds, new heroes, theme songs, and even a song about the game they just made are all earned.

The app is free. There is nothing to buy inside, and there never will be.

Questions

Is this real?

Yes. The app is being tested with kids right now on iPad and iPhone. Joining the list gets you early access before it's on the App Store.

How does it adapt to my kid?

It watches how they answer, not who they are. Fast, correct answers on hard questions move the difficulty up. Misses move it down. Answering before the voice finishes reading tells us they can read. No camera, no microphone, and nothing leaves the device.

What ages?

Three to seven. Pre-readers can build games alone because everything is pictures. Challenges scale with age.

Really no in-app purchases?

Really. Upgrades are unlocked by learning, and that will never change.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

Only to bake new games at home. Playing works fully offline, which is the whole point for car rides.
